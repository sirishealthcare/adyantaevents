About Us

About Adyanta Events - Hyderabad's Premier Event Planners

Adyanta Events is Hyderabad's leading event planning company, specializing in creating unforgettable weddings, corporate events, and celebrations since 2015. Our journey began with a passion for perfection and a deep connection to the art of event design.

In the quest to find the perfect event planner, our founder was left feeling disheartened. Determined to create a magical experience, she took matters into her own hands, pouring heart and soul into planning and designing every intricate detail.

From the fragrant blossoms to the enchanting lighting, the chairs carefully chosen, and the stage adorned with love, the vision became a reality. The overwhelming praise and heartfelt gratitude from loved ones sparked a flame, and Adyanta Events emerged, born out of passion and dedication.

With boundless dedication, our expert team strives to curate unforgettable events that touch the very essence of your soul. Your dreams become our purpose as we breathe life into them, weaving moments that will forever be etched in your heart. We serve Hyderabad, Telangana, and destinations across India.

— Adyanta Events
